Worth knowing

  • Height is the real filter. Only 11″ cube units and single-row drawer stands clear 24″. Every 13″ cube horizontal and every two-row fabric dresser lands at 26″ or taller, and so does nearly every design-brand shallow console.
  • Two near misses. The Latitude Run Dessein console at 70.7 × 13.7 × 11.7 has the best geometry found anywhere but costs $190. The Nicehill 5-drawer at 39 × 12 × 24 has the best fabric quality but is out of stock.
  • Not covered. Home Depot and Lowe's blocked every product page. Costco and Crate & Barrel had nothing under $150.
  • Bin sizes. IRONMIX, Mainstays, and ClosetMaid-style units take 11″ bins. Better Homes & Gardens and Target Brightroom 13″ units take 13″ bins.
